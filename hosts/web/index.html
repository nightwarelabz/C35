One procedural island, generated from a fixed seed — no image assets. The same engine crate renders natively and here. The camera holds the hero pose until the status reads “ready”, then begins its slow drift.

This page is lit by the real world: the sun, the sky and the season on screen are computed from your system clock and — if you grant it — your coarse geolocation, through a real solar model. If it is fall where you are, the foliage ambers; if it is 9 PM, the sun is down. There are no time or season controls here, by design. Your location is used locally by the engine and is never transmitted anywhere; only latitude/longitude feed the solar math. Deny the permission and the page says so above, and runs at a stated equator fallback instead.
